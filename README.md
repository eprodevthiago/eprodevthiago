## `01` — About me

I'm a Production Engineer focused on building software that solves real-world problems in industry and engineering.

My approach combines process improvement, industrial operations, software development, and business understanding to turn manual workflows into practical digital solutions.

Currently exploring:

- **Industrial SaaS** — production indicators, business rules, and multi-tenant architecture.
- **Full-stack development** — TypeScript, Next.js, PostgreSQL, and Supabase.
- **Artificial intelligence** — computer vision applied to structural inspection.
- **Application Security (AppSec)** — building more secure applications from the ground up.

> Engineering mindset. Software-driven solutions. Real-world problems.
